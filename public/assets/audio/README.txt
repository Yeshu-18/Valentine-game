Place your custom audio/video tracks in this folder (`public/assets/audio/`):

1. Grand Piano ("En Kadhale"):
   - /public/assets/audio/en-kadhale.mp4  (or en-kadhale.mp3)

2. Acoustic Guitar ("Thuli Thuli"):
   - /public/assets/audio/thuli-thuli.mp4 (or thuli-thuli.mp3)

When present, interacting with the Grand Piano or Acoustic Guitar in the Floor 1 Music Lounge (pressing E or Space) will automatically play these tracks alongside the animated keys, strings, and on-screen melody guide. If a file is not present yet, the game gracefully falls back to synthesized Web Audio notes.
