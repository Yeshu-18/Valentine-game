# 💙 HEARTLINE COASTER
### Complete Attraction Design

The coaster occupies the **northeast Spectacular Thrill Zone**. The entire attraction is one cohesive environment: **entrance → ticketing → queue → station → blue coaster → landscaped garden → signature reveals → return station**.

The overall visual language is:

**Deep blue + sapphire + cyan crystal + pearl white + tiny champagne-gold accents**

Pink should only appear as a romantic accent, not the main coaster color.

---

# 1. Overall Layout

Think of the attraction as a large landscaped loop.

```text
                         NORTH
                           ↑

                 ┌───────────────────┐
                 │   HIGH CREST      │
                 │        ↓          │
                 │   I LOVE YOU ❤️   │
                 │        ↓          │
                 │    MAJOR DROP     │
                 │        ╲          │
                 │         ╲         │
       CRYSTAL    │          ╲        │
       TUNNEL ←───┤           ╲       │
                 │            CASTLE │
                 │             FLYBY │
                 │                  ╲ │
                 │    HEARTLINE      ╲
                 │       ELEMENT      │
                 │                    │
                 │    GARDEN RUN      │
                 │         ╲          │
                 │       HELIX        │
                 │         ╲          │
                 └────── BRAKE ───────┘
                           │
                        STATION
                           │
                    QUEUE / EXIT
                           │
                   TICKET ENTRANCE
```

The important part is that **the coaster does not simply circle the castle**.

It has its own territory.

The castle can appear dramatically in the distance, but the coaster never physically cuts through or collides with Dream Castle.

---

# 2. Attraction Entrance

The entrance should be visible from the main park path.

### Entrance architecture

A large but elegant gateway:

- Two tall blue crystal pillars
- Pearl-white structural frame
- Sapphire glass panels
- Small champagne-gold details
- Large heart-shaped coaster emblem
- Blue illuminated lettering:

**HEARTLINE**

Underneath:

**THE SKY OF OUR DREAMS**

Keep the signage relatively restrained.

No giant theme-park clutter.

### Entrance shape

The gateway should have a slightly curved architectural profile.

```text
        ╭──────────────╮
      ╱                  ╲
     │      ♥            │
     │    HEARTLINE      │
     │  THE SKY OF OUR   │
     │      DREAMS       │
      ╲                  ╱
        ╰──────────────╯
```

The heart isn't pink neon.

It is a **small glowing sapphire crystal heart**.

---

# 3. Ticket Entrance Plaza

Immediately after the gateway:

### Small forecourt

Use:

- blue stone paving
- pearl marble borders
- low manicured hedges
- blue flowers
- tiny crystal lamps
- benches
- decorative water channel
- coaster visible above the trees

The player should immediately understand:

> "This is where I enter the coaster."

---

# 4. Ticket Booth

Instead of a huge ticket building, create a compact luxury pavilion.

### Architecture

A small circular/octagonal structure:

- pearl stone base
- curved blue-glass roof
- sapphire crystal central ornament
- champagne-gold trim
- transparent windows

Inside:

- ticket counter
- small screen
- ticket scanner
- staff area
- decorative coaster model

### Interaction

Player approaches:

**[E] Ride Heartline**

Then:

**Ticket confirmed**

Then the queue opens.

No giant UI.

---

# 5. Queue

The queue should itself be an attraction.

Don't make:

> giant empty snake-shaped barriers.

Instead create a **beautiful garden queue**.

### Queue path

```text
Entrance
   ↓
Ticket
   ↓
Garden path
   ↓
Crystal arch
   ↓
Coaster viewing platform
   ↓
Blue garden
   ↓
Station
```

Use short sections of queue rather than endless zigzags.

---

# 6. Queue Garden

This is the first major garden.

### Plants

Use:

- blue hydrangea-style flowers
- white roses
- lavender flowers
- small pink flowers
- trimmed green shrubs
- ornamental grasses
- small flowering trees
- low hedges

### Important

The garden should remain mostly:

**green + blue + white**

Pink is only occasional.

---

# 7. Queue Viewing Deck

Create a small elevated viewing platform.

From here the player can see:

- coaster train
- station
- high track
- first climb
- distant castle
- garden below

A section of blue track passes overhead.

This gives the player a preview of the attraction before riding.

---

# 8. Station

The station should feel premium.

### Station architecture

Large open structure with:

- pearl-white columns
- dark blue structural beams
- sapphire glass
- cyan crystal lighting
- champagne-gold details
- translucent canopy

No giant enclosed building.

The coaster should remain visually exposed.

---

# 9. Station Platform

Two platforms:

### Boarding side

Train arrives.

### Exit side

Passengers leave after the ride.

Keep the physical layout extremely clear.

```text
QUEUE
  ↓
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│       BOARDING PLATFORM      │
├──────────────────────────────┤
│        BLUE TRAIN            │
├──────────────────────────────┤
│        TRACK                 │
└──────────────────────────────┘
             ↓
            EXIT
```

---

# 10. Roller-Coaster Train

This is where the blue identity becomes strongest.

### Overall shape

A sleek fantasy coaster train.

Not bulky.

Not futuristic spaceship-like.

More like an elegant high-speed vehicle.

### Colors

**70% deep/sapphire blue**

**20% pearl white**

**10% cyan/crystal + tiny gold accents**

---

## Front car

The nose is aerodynamic.

At the center:

### Crystal heart emblem

Small sapphire heart.

It glows softly.

Two narrow blue light strips run toward the sides.

---

## Seats

Each car:

**2 seats**

Total:

**4 cars**

**8 riders**

Each seat has:

- pearl-white shell
- blue upholstery
- blue shoulder restraints
- small crystal side accents

---

# 11. Train Undercarriage

Do not leave the train floating above the track.

Show:

- wheels
- wheel housings
- suspension
- undercarriage
- blue mechanical chassis

The wheels should visibly follow the rails.

This makes the train feel physically connected to the coaster.

---

# 12. Track Design

The track itself is the star.

### Rails

Two main rails:

- deep sapphire-blue structural rail
- metallic blue highlight

### Cross members

Dark blue/blue-gray.

### Supports

Mostly:

**deep navy blue**

with occasional:

**pearl-white support sections**

Avoid rainbow-colored supports.

---

# 14. Departure

Train leaves station slowly.

Blue station lights activate progressively.

Then:

**3... 2... 1...**

The train accelerates.

Don't display a countdown UI.

The environment itself provides the anticipation.

---

# 15. Lift Hill

The first major vertical structure.

Large blue supports.

Track climbs above the surrounding gardens.

As the train rises, the player gradually sees:

1. garden
2. coaster
3. park
4. castle
5. island
6. horizon

The climb should feel like the world is opening underneath them.

---

# 16. The Crest

This is the most important physical moment.

The train reaches the highest point.

Instead of immediately dropping:

**slow down.**

Almost creep forward.

Then the camera naturally looks downward.

---

# 17. The Garden Below

This is the signature landscape.

Directly below the crest:

### Giant landscaped meadow

Not a flat green plane.

Use:

- gently rolling terrain
- grass
- flowers
- bushes
- trees
- curved flower borders
- small paths
- decorative rocks

And hidden inside the landscaping:

# I LOVE YOU ❤️

But **not as floating text**.

The words are physically created through the landscape.

---

# 18. How "I LOVE YOU ❤️" Is Built

The letters should be created through:

- shorter grass
- darker/lighter grass regions
- carefully shaped terrain
- flower borders
- trimmed vegetation
- subtle soil variation

From normal ground level it looks like landscaping.

From the coaster crest it becomes readable.

Example:

```text
      I   LOVE   YOU

             ♥
```

The heart should also be made from landscaping.

No billboard.

No hologram.

No giant particle text.

No UI.

No floating letters.

---

# 19. Love Reveal Camera

The train slows.

Camera gradually tilts downward.

First:

**grass**

Then:

**patterns**

Then:

**letters**

Then:

# I LOVE YOU ❤️

Hold approximately **1.5 to 2 seconds**.

No dialogue.

No fireworks.

No giant effects.

Just the landscape.

Then the train rolls over the crest.

---

# 20. Major Drop

Immediately after the emotional pause:

**DROP.**

The track dives dramatically.

The train accelerates down toward the lower park.

Use:

- strong wind
- subtle camera vibration
- increasing FOV
- blue track streaks
- environmental motion

No excessive screen shake.

---

# 21. Low Turn

At the bottom:

A fast sweeping blue track turn.

The train passes:

- flowers
- trees
- blue crystal structures
- small bridges

The track should feel close to the environment.

---

# 22. Castle Flyby

This is the biggest architectural reveal.

The train exits the turn.

Dream Castle appears.

First:

**one tower**

Then:

**multiple towers**

Then:

**the whole castle**

The coaster passes close enough to feel spectacular, but remains outside the castle's collision/exclusion zone.

The castle should visually dominate the horizon.

The coaster then banks away.

---

# 23. Castle-Side Garden

Between the coaster and castle:

- manicured lawn
- roses
- blue flowers
- crystal trees
- small fountains
- decorative pathways

This creates a visual buffer.

It also prevents the coaster from looking like it is cutting through the castle.

---

# 24. Crystal Approach

The environment gradually changes.

First:

small crystals

Then:

larger crystal clusters

Then:

crystal arches

Then:

a complete crystal corridor.

Colors:

**blue → cyan → lavender**

---

# 25. Crystal Tunnel

The train enters the tunnel.

This should be one of the most visually beautiful parts.

The walls are made from translucent crystals.

As the train passes:

```text
Train
 ↓
Crystal 1 → ON
Crystal 2 → ON
Crystal 3 → ON
Crystal 4 → ON
Crystal 5 → ON
```

Lighting appears to travel with the train.

The blue train reflects in the crystal surfaces.

Small floating particles are allowed, but keep them subtle.

---

# 26. Heartline Element

After exiting the crystal tunnel:

The track forms the coaster's signature shape.

From outside:

A subtle **heart-inspired silhouette**.

Inside the ride:

It feels like:

- banking
- rising
- turning
- diving
- changing elevation

Don't make a literal giant heart-shaped track that feels gimmicky.

It should be elegant.

---

# 27. Garden Speed Run

Now the coaster becomes extremely fast.

Track stays relatively low.

The train races through a carefully designed garden.

Sequence:

**Blue arch**

↓

**flower garden**

↓

**small bridge**

↓

**crystal garden**

↓

**trees**

↓

**water feature**

↓

**stone garden**

↓

**blue crystal arch**

This gives the ride visual rhythm.

---

# 28. Water Feature

A small stream runs underneath part of the coaster.

The track crosses over it.

Water should reflect:

- blue train
- blue track
- crystal lighting

Keep it physically plausible.

---

# 29. Coaster Bridge

Create one signature bridge.

Blue structural frame.

Pearl stone foundations.

The track crosses above a garden path.

Players elsewhere in the park can see the train passing overhead.

This makes the attraction feel alive even when you're not riding it.

---

# 30. Final Helix

The track begins climbing again.

Large sweeping spiral.

The train slows gradually.

The environment opens.

Now the player sees:

- entire coaster
- garden
- park
- Dream Castle
- island
- sky

This is the final panoramic moment.

---

# 31. Second View of the Message

From the helix, the landscaped:

**I LOVE YOU ❤️**

can appear far away.

It should not be emphasized as strongly as the first reveal.

It's simply a beautiful callback.

---

# 32. Final World Reveal

At the highest point of the helix:

The train looks outward.

The whole park becomes visible.

The camera should remain physically attached to the train.

No camera teleport.

No cinematic cut.

The world simply unfolds naturally.

---

# 33. Brake Run

After the helix:

Track straightens.

Train gradually slows.

Lighting becomes calmer.

Audio decreases.

Mechanical coaster sounds become more noticeable.

Then:

**station.**

---

# 34. Station Arrival

Train enters the station.

Blue lights pulse softly.

Train stops.

Restraints release.

Player regains normal movement.

No giant:

> 🎉 YOU WON!

screen.

The attraction simply returns control to the player.

---

# 35. Exit Garden

After leaving the station:

Create a separate garden path.

It should pass beside the coaster.

Players can see trains arriving and departing.

Add:

- benches
- flowers
- blue crystal lamps
- small viewing points

---

# 36. Exit Plaza

The exit returns naturally to the main park.

Include a small sign:

**HEARTLINE**

with an arrow pointing back toward the attraction.

No giant advertising wall.

---

# 37. Attraction at Night

At night, the coaster becomes a major landmark.

The track glows subtly blue.

Not neon everywhere.

Use:

- sapphire edge lights
- cyan crystal highlights
- blue station lighting
- occasional white accents

The train has tiny blue running lights.

From far away, the coaster becomes a glowing blue ribbon through the park.

---

# 38. Garden Lighting

The gardens should remain darker than the coaster.

Use small:

- ground lights
- crystal lamps
- flower illumination
- path lights

This creates hierarchy:

**Coaster = brightest**

**Garden = supporting**

**Castle = major distant landmark**

---

# 39. What Players See From Outside

This is important.

The coaster must still feel alive when the player isn't riding.

From the park:

- train periodically travels the track
- lift hill is visible
- drop is visible
- crystal tunnel glows
- helix is visible
- station is active
- garden is alive

The coaster becomes part of the park's ambient movement.

# 🎨 Final Color Palette

| Element | Color |
|---|---|
| Main track | Sapphire / royal blue |
| Track highlights | Electric blue |
| Supports | Deep navy |
| Train | Pearl white + blue |
| Train glass | Cyan |
| Station | Pearl + sapphire |
| Crystal tunnel | Cyan / lavender |
| Garden | Natural green |
| Flowers | Blue / white / small pink |
| Gold | Champagne, very limited |
| Castle | Pink/lavender/gold |
| Love heart | Natural red |
| Night coaster | Blue glow |

### The key visual rule

**The coaster itself is BLUE.**

The **garden is green and natural.**

The **castle remains pink/lavender/gold.**

The **crystal section is cyan/lavender.**

That separation will make the entire park much easier to read visually. 💙

And most importantly, the coaster should feel like a **real attraction physically embedded into the landscape**, rather than a decorative track floating above the park.
